Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

1. Introduction

Vaultly ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our content scheduling and publishing platform.

2. Information We Collect

When you sign in with TikTok, we access the following data through TikTok's official APIs:

  • Profile Information: Display name, username, avatar, and bio (via user.info.basic and user.info.profile)
  • Account Statistics: Follower count, following count, likes count, and video count (via user.info.stats)
  • Video Data: Your public video information including views, likes, comments, and shares (via video.list)

3. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used to power Vaultly's features: displaying your profile overview, analyzing video performance to suggest optimal posting times, and populating your content library. We do not use your data for purposes other than providing and improving the Service.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data or TikTok information with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes. Data may be disclosed only if required by law or to protect our legal rights.

5. Data Storage and Security

We store your data using industry-standard encryption and security practices. Data is retained only while your account is active. Upon account deletion, your data is permanently removed from our systems within 30 days.

6. TikTok Data

Our access to your TikTok data is governed by TikTok's Developer Terms of Service. We access data only with your explicit authorization through Login Kit. You may revoke access at any time via your TikTok account settings.

7. Your Rights

You may access, correct, or delete your data at any time. Disconnect your TikTok account to remove your data from Vaultly. Contact us at support@vaultly.app for data requests.

8. Cookies

We use essential cookies for session management. No third-party advertising or tracking cookies are used.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. The "Last updated" date reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the Service constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
