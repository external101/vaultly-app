Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about Vaultly.

How does Vaultly connect to my TikTok account?
Vaultly uses TikTok's official Login Kit to connect securely. You authorize access through TikTok's own sign-in page, and we never see your password. We request permission to view your profile, follower stats, and video data.
What data does Vaultly access?
We access your basic profile info (display name, avatar), follower and engagement stats, and your public video list. This powers your analytics dashboard and scheduling recommendations. We do not post or modify anything on your account.
Is my data secure?
Yes. All connections use HTTPS encryption. We never sell or share your data with third parties. You can disconnect your account and delete all stored data at any time from your settings. See our Privacy Policy for details.
Can I schedule posts directly to TikTok?
Vaultly's calendar lets you plan your content schedule. When it's time to post, you receive a notification with your pre-written caption and media ready to go. Direct publishing depends on TikTok API availability for your account type.
How do I cancel my subscription?
You can cancel your Pro plan anytime from your account settings. Your plan stays active until the end of your billing period, then reverts to Free. No cancellation fees or hidden charges.
